About Me

My name is Darby M. Duran, a dedicated frontend web developer. I started learning frontend development in high school, where I began experimenting with small web projects — tinkering with layouts, breaking things, and figuring out how to fix them. That early curiosity turned into a genuine interest in building websites and interactive experiences, and it's what keeps me writing code today.

I work primarily with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I'm still early in my journey as a developer. I'm upfront about that: I'm a junior frontend developer who learns by building — through personal projects, experimentation, and a fair amount of debugging. What I bring to the table is genuine enthusiasm for the craft and a growing set of real projects to show for it.

I enjoy building responsive websites, creating interactive interfaces, and finding small ways to improve how a page feels to use. Turning an idea into something that actually runs in a browser is still the part I like most, and I'm always looking for the next thing to learn.
